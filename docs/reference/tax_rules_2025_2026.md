# Tax rules for 2025 and 2026: what the calculator applies and why

Last checked: 02/10/2026. Tax year 2025 is income earned in 2025 (returns filed in 2026); tax year 2026 is income earned in 2026 under Law 5246/2025 (ΦΕΚ Α' 198/11.11.2025).

**How this was verified.** The primary texts (taxheaven.gr, aade.gr, e-nomothesia.gr, efka.gov.gr) could not be fetched from the environment used for this review, so each figure below was checked through search-engine extracts of those pages and cross-checked between at least two sources, or against a published worked example. Figures marked *open* could not be settled that way.

## Income tax scale (Article 15 ΚΦΕ)

| | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10,000 | 9% | 9% (0% with 4+ children) |
| 10,000-20,000 | 22% | 20%; 18/16/9% with 1/2/3 children; 0% with 4+ |
| 20,000-30,000 | 28% | 26%, less 2 points per dependent child (24% with 1 ... 14% with 6, 12% with 7), down to 0% |
| 30,000-40,000 | 36% | 34% |
| 40,000-60,000 | 44% | 39% |
| above 60,000 | 44% | 44% |

The same scale applies to salaries, pensions, business (freelance) and agricultural income. The 20,000-30,000 reduction continues beyond four children: the rate "μειώνεται περαιτέρω κατά δύο ποσοστιαίες μονάδες (2%) για κάθε επιπλέον εξαρτώμενο τέκνο άνω των τεσσάρων" (Law 5246/2025 art. 3, as communicated by AADE circular Ο.3068/2025). Where an age-based and a child-based rate both apply, the lower one is used.

**Youth rates, 2026 only:** up to and including 25 years old, 0% up to 20,000; aged 26-30, 9% on the 10,000-20,000 band. Age is taken as the tax year minus the year of birth (for 2026, born in 2001 or later counts as up to 25); this follows secondary sources, as the circular's wording on the reference date could not be read. There were no youth rates in 2025: the published 2025-2026 comparisons show under-25s paying the normal 9%/22%. The calculator applies them to salary, freelance and agricultural income.

Sources: [AADE circular Ο.3068/2025](https://www.aade.gr/egkyklioi-kai-apofaseis/o-3068-18-11-2025), [Law 5246/2025 (ΦΕΚ Α' 198/2025), AADE copy](https://www.aade.gr/sites/default/files/2025-11/%CE%91%CE%A0%CE%9F%CE%A3%CE%A0%CE%91%CE%A3%CE%9C%CE%91%20%CE%A6%CE%95%CE%9A%20%CE%91%20198_2025%20%CE%9D%205246_2025.pdf), [taxheaven: 2026 scales compared with 2025](https://www.taxheaven.gr/news/71602/oi-nees-analytikes-klimakes-forologias-2026-sygkrish-2025-2026), [taxheaven: 2025 scales](https://www.taxheaven.gr/news/72180/klimakes-forologias-2025), [KPMG tax update 20/11/2025](https://kpmg.com/gr/el/home/insights/2025/11/tax-updates-20112025.html), [powergame.gr on youth taxation](https://www.powergame.gr/forologia/1250560/anasa-gia-tous-neous-ergazomenous-apo-to-2026-poso-meionontai-oi-foroi-gia-poious-midenizontai/), [cnn.gr on the 2026 changes](https://www.cnn.gr/oikonomia/chrima/story/504395/pro-ton-pylon-meioseis-foron-ypsous-1-5-dis-ti-allazei-se-tekmiria-enoikia-kai-forologisi-neon).

## Tax reduction (Article 16 ΚΦΕ)

- 777 / 900 / 1,120 / 1,340 / 1,580 / 1,780 for 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 dependent children, plus 220 per child beyond the fifth (from tax year 2024).
- It reduces the tax on **salaries and pensions**, and on agricultural income of **professional farmers** (κατ' επάγγελμα αγρότες). It never offsets tax on freelance, rental, investment or other income.
- Above 12,000 of **taxable** salary, pension and (for professional farmers) agricultural income, it falls by 20 per 1,000, pro rata. Taxable means after employee contributions and after the Article 5Γ exemption. There is no reduction with 5 or more children.

Checks: the published 2026 farmer examples (no children, 15,000: tax 1,183; two children, 20,000: tax 1,540) and an external comparison against aftertax.gr across employment scenarios for 2025 and 2026 both match to the cent; see `tests/unit/test_salary_credit_phase_out.py`.

Sources: [taxheaven: Article 16](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/4172/2013/arthro/16), [forin: Article 16 for salaries and pensions](https://www.forin.gr/articles/article/84626/meiwsh-forou-tou-arthrou-16-tou-kfe-gia-to-eisodhma-apo-misthwth-ergasia-kai-suntakseis), [ΔΕΔ Θ 573/2021 on farmers](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/37155/ded-o-573-2021), [farmers' examples](https://www.agrotypos.gr/oi-allages-stin-forologia-ton-agroton-pou-tha-ischysoun-gia-diloseis-forologikou-etous-2026).

## Expense credits

- **Donations (Article 19):** 20% of donations, only if they exceed 100 in the year; "το συνολικό ποσό των δωρεών δεν μπορεί να υπερβαίνει το 5% του φορολογητέου εισοδήματος", so the cap applies to the donations, not to the credit. Not modelled: a 40% rate reported for donations to charitable foundations and registered civil-society organisations.
- **Medical expenses:** 10% of the amount above 5% of income, up to 3,000.
- There is **no** credit for tuition or for life and health insurance premiums: both were abolished when Law 4172/2013 took effect on 01/01/2014.

Sources: [taxheaven: Article 19](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/4172/2013/arthro/19), [in.gr on the 2014 code](https://www.in.gr/2013/10/03/economy/ekptwsi-foroy-ws-3-000-eyrw-gia-iatrika-eksoda/).

## Rental income (Article 40 ΚΦΕ)

- 2025: 15% up to 12,000; 35% to 35,000; 45% above.
- 2026: 15% up to 12,000; 25% to 24,000; 35% to 36,000; 45% above. The law text sets 36,000 ("τριάντα έξι χιλιάδες"); news sites that give 35,000 repeat the pre-2026 boundary.

Sources: [taxheaven: Article 8 of Law 5246/2025](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5246/2025/arthro/8), [taxrevenue.gr on the 2026 scale](https://taxrevenue.gr/forologia-enoikion-poso-foro-plirono/).

## Social security contributions

- Salaried employees: employee 13.37%, employer 21.79% (2025 and 2026); monthly ceiling 7,572.62 in 2025 and 7,761.94 in 2026 (+2.5%, e-EFKA circular 4/2026).
- Freelancers (non-salaried), monthly main pension and health contribution:

| Class | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | Reduced (new) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 244.65 | 293.59 | 351.84 | 422.90 | 506.78 | 659.39 | 146.79 |
| 2026 | 250.77 | 300.93 | 360.63 | 433.47 | 519.45 | 675.87 | 150.46 |

- Engineers pay the same main classes plus auxiliary (2026: 46.57 / 56.13 / 66.88) and lump-sum (2026: 31.05 / 37.02 / 44.18) classes 1-3; 2025 amounts are 2.5% lower.

Sources: [e-EFKA circular 6/2026](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/52023/egkyklios-e-efka-6-2026), [e-EFKA circular 4/2026](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/51989/egkyklios-e-efka-4-2026), [e-EFKA circular 2/2025](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/49513/egkyklios-e-efka-2-2025).

## Other

- Investment income: dividends 5%, interest 15%, capital gains 15%, royalties 20%.
- Business activity fee (τέλος επιτηδεύματος): abolished for individuals from tax year 2025 (Law 5162/2024).
- Tax residency transfer (Article 5Γ): 50% of Greek salary or business income exempt for 7 years.

## Open or not modelled

- **Youth rates:** the circular's wording on how age is counted, and whether pensions qualify, could not be read; the calculator applies the rates to salary, freelance and agricultural income only.
- **Professional farmers, 0% up to 20,000 from tax year 2026:** announced on 05-07/09/2026 and still awaiting legislation ("με νομοθετική ρύθμιση που θα έρθει στη Βουλή"); not modelled until enacted. The published examples imply the 9% and 20% bands become 0% on the first 20,000 while the Article 16 reduction stays.
- **Minimum presumed income for freelancers (Articles 28Α-28Δ ΚΦΕ):** not modelled, so tax for low-profit freelancers can be understated. Its base is the annual minimum wage x 14 (12,320 for 2025, 12,880 for 2026), raised 10% per three years of activity, plus 10% of payroll cost and 5% of turnover above the activity code's average, capped at 50,000; the first three years are exempt and several reductions apply. The removal of the payroll and turnover components for compliant taxpayers was announced in September 2026 and is not yet law.
- **Electronic spending requirement** (30% of income, 22% on any shortfall): not modelled.
- **Interest on listed corporate bonds** at 5% (from 11/04/2025): not modelled; all interest uses 15%.
