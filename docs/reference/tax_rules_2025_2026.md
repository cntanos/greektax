# Tax rules for 2025 and 2026: what the calculator applies and why

Last checked: 20/11/2026. Tax year 2025 is income earned in 2025 (returns filed in 2026); tax year 2026 is income earned in 2026 under Law 5246/2025 (ΦΕΚ Α' 198/11.11.2025) and Law 5355/2026 (ΦΕΚ Α' 188/13.11.2026).

**How this was verified.** The primary texts (taxheaven.gr, aade.gr, e-nomothesia.gr, efka.gov.gr) could not be fetched from the environment used for this review, so each figure below was checked through search-engine extracts of those pages and cross-checked between at least two sources, or against a published worked example. Figures marked *open* could not be settled that way.

## Income tax scale (Article 15 ΚΦΕ)

| | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10,000 | 9% | 9% (0% with 4+ children) |
| 10,000-20,000 | 22% | 20%; 18/16/9% with 1/2/3 children; 0% with 4+ |
| 20,000-30,000 | 28% | 26%; 24/22/20/18% with 1-4 children, then 2 points less per further child (16% with 5 ... 0% with 13) |
| 30,000-40,000 | 36% | 34% |
| 40,000-60,000 | 44% | 39% |
| above 60,000 | 44% | 44% |

The same scale applies to salaries, pensions, business (freelance) and agricultural income.

**Agricultural profit is taxed on its own (Article 29 par. 3 ΚΦΕ).** Salaries, pensions and business profit are added together before the scale is applied; profit from an individual agricultural business goes through the scale separately, so it starts again at the first band. The calculator applies this in every year.

**Professional farmers, from tax year 2026 (Article 4 of Law 5355/2026):** 0% on the first two bands (up to 20,000) of agricultural profit, for professional farmers, "provided that at least 50% of their income comes from agricultural activity" (Art. 15(1)(στ)). The calculator applies it whenever the professional-farmer box is ticked and does not test the 50% condition. Farmers aged 26-30 get the 0%, not the 9% youth rate. The Article 16 reduction still applies above that. Checks: the published examples (no children, 30,000: tax 2,183 instead of 5,083; two children, 20,000: 0 instead of 1,540; aged 28 with one child, 19,000: 0 instead of 950; tax-free amount without children 22,204) all match; see `tests/unit/test_salary_credit_phase_out.py`.

**Youth rates, 2026 only:** up to and including 25 years old, 0% up to 20,000; aged 26-30, 9% on the 10,000-20,000 band. There were no youth rates in 2025: the published 2025-2026 comparisons show under-25s paying the normal 9%/22%. The calculator applies them to salary, freelance and agricultural income.

Sources: [taxheaven: 2026 scales compared with 2025](https://www.taxheaven.gr/news/71602/oi-nees-analytikes-klimakes-forologias-2026-sygkrish-2025-2026), [taxheaven: 2025 scales](https://www.taxheaven.gr/news/72180/klimakes-forologias-2025), [KPMG tax update 20/11/2025](https://kpmg.com/gr/el/home/insights/2025/11/tax-updates-20112025.html), [powergame.gr on youth taxation](https://www.powergame.gr/forologia/1250560/anasa-gia-tous-neous-ergazomenous-apo-to-2026-poso-meionontai-oi-foroi-gia-poious-midenizontai/), [cnn.gr on the 2026 changes](https://www.cnn.gr/oikonomia/chrima/story/504395/pro-ton-pylon-meioseis-foron-ypsous-1-5-dis-ti-allazei-se-tekmiria-enoikia-kai-forologisi-neon), [taxheaven: Law 5355/2026](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5355/2026), [taxheaven: farmers and three-child families under the new scale](https://www.taxheaven.gr/news/74548/agrotes-kai-triteknoi-h-nea-forologikh-klimaka-kai-to-ofelos-ana-eisodhma), [agrotypos: zero rate for farm income up to 20,000](https://www.agrotypos.gr/chrima/forologia/stin-vouli-o-midenikos-syntelestis-forologisis-sta-agrotika-eisodimata-eos-20000-evro), [Article 29 ΚΦΕ](https://www.technologismiki.com/nomos/n_4172_13_29.php).

The consolidated text of Article 15(1) ΚΦΕ as it stands after Law 5355/2026 was read in full on [taxheaven: Article 4 of Law 5355/2026](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5355/2026/arthro/4) (checked 20/11/2026). It confirms every 2026 rate above, and that the 20,000-30,000 rate keeps falling by 2 points for each child above four (Art. 15(1)(δε)). The calculator applies 0% from 13 children on, which is where that formula reaches 0; the text does not say what happens beyond it.

## Tax reduction (Article 16 ΚΦΕ)

- 777 / 900 / 1,120 / 1,340 / 1,580 / 1,780 for 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 dependent children, plus 220 per child beyond the fifth (from tax year 2024).
- It reduces the tax on **salaries and pensions**, and on agricultural income of **professional farmers** (κατ' επάγγελμα αγρότες). It never offsets tax on freelance, rental, investment or other income.
- Above 12,000 of **taxable** salary, pension and (for professional farmers) agricultural income, it falls by 20 per 1,000, pro rata. Taxable means after employee contributions and after the Article 5Γ exemption. There is no reduction with 5 or more children.

Checks: the farmer examples published before Law 5355/2026 (no children, 15,000: tax 1,183; two children, 20,000: tax 1,540) matched until that law's 0% rate replaced them, and an external comparison against aftertax.gr across employment scenarios for 2025 and 2026 both match to the cent; see `tests/unit/test_salary_credit_phase_out.py`.

When a professional farmer also has a salary or pension, the reduction is sized once on the two taxable incomes together and shared in proportion to their tax.

Sources: [taxheaven: Article 16](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/4172/2013/arthro/16), [forin: Article 16 for salaries and pensions](https://www.forin.gr/articles/article/84626/meiwsh-forou-tou-arthrou-16-tou-kfe-gia-to-eisodhma-apo-misthwth-ergasia-kai-suntakseis), [ΔΕΔ Θ 573/2021 on farmers](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/37155/ded-o-573-2021), [farmers' examples](https://www.agrotypos.gr/oi-allages-stin-forologia-ton-agroton-pou-tha-ischysoun-gia-diloseis-forologikou-etous-2026).

## Expense credits

- **Donations (Article 19):** 20% of donations, only if they exceed 100 in the year; donations count up to 5% of taxable income.
- **Medical expenses:** 10% of the amount above 5% of income, up to 3,000.
- There is **no** credit for tuition or for life and health insurance premiums: both were abolished when Law 4172/2013 took effect on 01/01/2014.

Sources: [taxheaven: Article 19](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/4172/2013/arthro/19), [in.gr on the 2014 code](https://www.in.gr/2013/10/03/economy/ekptwsi-foroy-ws-3-000-eyrw-gia-iatrika-eksoda/).

## Rental income (Article 40 ΚΦΕ)

- 2025: 15% up to 12,000; 35% to 35,000; 45% above.
- 2026: 15% up to 12,000; 25% to 24,000; 35% to 36,000; 45% above. Several news sites give 35,000 for the last boundary; Article 8 of Law 5246/2025 and AADE circular Ο.3068/2025 give 36,000, which the calculator uses.

Sources: [taxheaven: Article 8 of Law 5246/2025](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5246/2025/arthro/8), [AADE circular Ο.3068/2025](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/51509/o-3068-2025), [taxrevenue.gr on the 2026 scale](https://taxrevenue.gr/forologia-enoikion-poso-foro-plirono/).

## Social security contributions

- Salaried employees: employee 13.37%, employer 21.79% (2025 and 2026); monthly ceiling 7,572.62 in 2025 and 7,761.94 in 2026 (+2.5%, e-EFKA circular 4/2026).
- Freelancers (non-salaried), monthly main pension and health contribution:

| Class | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | Reduced (new) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 244.65 | 293.59 | 351.84 | 422.90 | 506.78 | 659.39 | 146.79 |
| 2026 | 250.77 | 300.93 | 360.63 | 433.47 | 519.45 | 675.87 | 150.46 |

- Engineers pay the same main classes plus auxiliary (2026: 46.57 / 56.13 / 66.88) and lump-sum (2026: 31.05 / 37.02 / 44.18) classes 1-3; 2025 amounts are 2.5% lower.

Sources: [e-EFKA circular 6/2026](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/52023/egkyklios-e-efka-6-2026), [e-EFKA circular 4/2026](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/51989/egkyklios-e-efka-4-2026), [e-EFKA circular 2/2025](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/49513/egkyklios-e-efka-2-2025).

## Other

- Investment income: dividends 5%, interest 15%, capital gains 15%, royalties 20%.
- Business activity fee (τέλος επιτηδεύματος): abolished for individuals from tax year 2025 (Law 5162/2024).
- Tax residency transfer (Article 5Γ): 50% of the income from salaried work and from individual business activity earned in Greece is exempt for 7 tax years. The calculator halves the taxable salary (after employee contributions) and the taxable freelance profit (after contributions); pensions, agricultural, rental, investment and other income are not affected. AADE decides each category separately, so the salary and freelance sections each have their own box. Source: [AADE questions and answers on Article 5Γ, 02/10/2025](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/51379/syxnes-erwthseis-apanthseis-ypobolh-aithshs-ypagwghs-se-eidiko-tropo-forologhshs-aroro-5g-toy-kfe).

## Open or not modelled

- **Youth rates:** the exact statutory definition of age, and whether pensions are covered.
- **Law 5355/2026, tax year 2026:** the higher tax-free limit for tips (6,000 a year) is not modelled; the calculator has no tips input.
- **Law 5355/2026, from tax year 2027:** 0% up to 20,000 for taxpayers with three children, and lower employee contributions from April 2027. They belong in the 2027 year file, which does not exist yet.
- **Minimum presumed income for freelancers (Article 28A ΚΦΕ)** and its exemptions: not modelled, so tax for low-profit freelancers can be understated.
- **Electronic spending requirement** (30% of income, 22% on any shortfall): not modelled.
- **Interest on listed corporate bonds** at 5% (from 11/04/2025): not modelled; all interest uses 15%.
